Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how PostmanPingBot ("we", "the service", or "the application"), developed by shovon.bd, accesses, processes, and protects your information when connecting to your Google Account.

1. Data We Access

PostmanPingBot requests the absolute minimum Google OAuth scope required to provide real-time sender alerting:

Authorized Scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.metadata

This restricted metadata scope allows the application to read only email header values (specifically the From, Subject, and Date headers) and message IDs.

What We DO NOT Access:

  • We never access, read, or process the body text, HTML content, or attachments of your emails.
  • We do not send emails, draft emails, modify inbox labels, or delete any messages.
  • We do not access your Google contacts or profile information beyond standard OAuth authentication identifiers.

2. How Your Data Is Used

When an inbound email arrives in your inbox, Google Cloud Pub/Sub sends a lightweight webhook to PostmanPingBot. The application:

  • Extracts the sender's address from the From header.
  • Compares the address or domain against your configured private watchlist.
  • If a match is found, transmits an instant alert containing the sender name, subject, and timestamp to your private Telegram chat.

3. Data Storage & Security

  • No Email Storage: Email headers, subjects, and contents are processed strictly in ephemeral serverless memory and are never stored in any database.
  • Encrypted Tokens: Your Google OAuth refresh token is encrypted at rest using industry-standard AES-256-GCM encryption.
  • Access Control: Telegram webhook interactions are cryptographically verified and restricted strictly to the designated bot owner.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

PostmanPingBot does not sell, rent, license, or share your data with any third parties, advertisers, or analytics brokers. Data is only communicated between Google APIs, Upstash Redis (encrypted token storage), and the Telegram Bot API for the sole purpose of delivering your alerts.

5. Google Limited Use Disclosure

PostmanPingBot's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Revoking Access & Data Deletion

You retain full control over your Google account permissions at all times. You can immediately revoke PostmanPingBot's access at any time through:

Google Account Security Permissions ➔ Select PostmanPingBot ➔ Click Remove Access.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy or PostmanPingBot, please contact the developer at shovon.bd.
